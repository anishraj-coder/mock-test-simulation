Exam Instructions & Navigation Rules

General Instructions:

  • The total duration of the test will be displayed on the countdown timer at the top.
  • When the timer reaches 00:00:00, the test will automatically conclude and submit.
  • You can navigate freely between questions and sections using the question palette or section tabs.

Palette Status Legend:

  • Grey: You have not visited the question yet.
  • Red: You have visited the question but have not answered it.
  • Green: You have answered the question.
  • Purple: You have marked the question for review without answering.
  • Purple with Green dot: You have answered the question and also marked it for review.
